Where the bay meets good company

Zulā Beach sits right on the water at Tuzigazi in Richards Bay. Barefoot lounges, shaded decks and golden sunsets over the harbour make it the perfect place to slow down.

From hearty breakfasts and flame-grilled meats to fresh seafood platters and ice-cold drinks, everything is made for sharing.
